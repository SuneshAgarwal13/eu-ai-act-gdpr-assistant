## Status of the Digital Omnibus on AI

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 amends the AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). It is adopted
law: provisional agreement between Parliament and Council on 7 May 2026, Parliament
plenary vote on 16 June 2026, Council adoption on 29 June 2026, signature on 8 July 2026
and publication in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 (procedure 2025/0359(COD)).
This assistant answers from the AI Act as consolidated by EUR-Lex on 27 July 2026,
which already includes these amendments.

## New application dates for high-risk AI systems

The amendment postpones the high-risk obligations in Chapter III, Sections 1 to 3.
They apply from 2 December 2027 for AI systems that are high-risk under Article 6(2)
and Annex III (stand-alone systems), and from 2 August 2028 for AI systems that are
high-risk under Article 6(1) and Annex I (AI in products covered by Union
harmonisation legislation). The authoritative dates are in Article 113 of the
consolidated AI Act.

## Other changes made by the amendment

The amendment adds a prohibition on AI practices that generate non-consensual intimate
or sexual content, including child sexual abuse material; inserts Article 4a on
processing special categories of personal data for bias detection and correction,
subject to a strict necessity standard; extends simplified documentation to
medium-sized companies; reinforces the AI Office's powers; and removes AI-enabled
machinery from the AI Act's direct high-risk regime (deleting the Machinery Directive
entry from Annex I) in favour of requirements under the Machinery Regulation. The
Article 50(2) marking obligation for AI-generated content applies from 2 December 2026
to systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026.
