## Status of the proposed GDPR amendments

In November 2025 the European Commission proposed amendments to the GDPR as part of its
Digital Omnibus package, including a narrower definition of personal data in
Article 4(1). As of 18 October 2026 these amendments are still being negotiated by the
European Parliament and the Council and have not been adopted. The GDPR applies as
originally enacted, and the GDPR text this assistant answers from is unamended.
Questions about what the final amended GDPR will say cannot be answered yet.
