## Status of the high-risk classification guidelines

Article 6(5) of the AI Act requires the Commission to publish guidelines on the
practical implementation of the high-risk classification rules, with examples of
high-risk and non-high-risk use cases. The Commission published draft guidelines for
consultation on 19 May 2026. As of 18 October 2026 no final version has been adopted,
so this assistant does not include them; classification questions are answered from
Article 6 and Annexes I and III of the AI Act.
